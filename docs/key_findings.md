# Key Findings (draft)

All figures are from a synthetic SACCO loan book (6,000 loans, Oct 2023 to Sep 2026).

1. **PAR30 rose from about 3% (2024) to 7.9% (Sep 2026)**, peaking at 9.5% in Jul 2026.
2. **PAR90 kept rising after PAR30 eased**: from 3.0% (May 2026) to 5.7% (Sep 2026), as loans that slipped in mid-2026 aged into the worst bucket. PAR30 alone would have suggested improvement.
3. **The 2025-Q2 vintage is the weakest cohort**: by age 6 months, 14.4% of its balance is 30+ days late, against 3% to 7% for 2024 cohorts at the same age.
